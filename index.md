## Research

My work focuses on structural properties of quantum operations.

Example:

The Choi matrix of a quantum channel $\mathcal{E}: A' \to A$ is

$$
J_{\mathcal{E}} = (\mathrm{id} \otimes \mathcal{E})(|\Phi^+\rangle\langle\Phi^+|).
$$

We study constraints such as:

$$
\operatorname{Tr}_A J_{\mathcal{E}} = \mathbb{I}_{A'}.
$$
