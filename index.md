## About

I am a Postdoctoral fellow at IIIT Hyderabad, working under the supervision of Dr. Siddhartha Das. I am fascinated by quantum physics and its potential in transforming the technology of the future. I work in quantum information theory, with interests in quantum thermodynamics, resource theories, and thermodynamic aspects of quantum processes. I completed my Ph.D. from IMSc Chennai in 2025, where I was advised by Dr. C. M. Chandrashekar and worked closely with Dr. Sibasish Ghosh. Before joining the doctoral program, I spent a year ('16-'17) in HRI Allahabad as a long-term visiting student, working with Dr. Dileep Jatkar on the aspects of de Sitter Cosmology. Prior to that, I received my BS-MS dual degree from IISER Pune. I was advised by Dr. Alok Laddha (CMI Chennai) for my Masters' thesis on qauntum field theory in de Sitter spacetime.
